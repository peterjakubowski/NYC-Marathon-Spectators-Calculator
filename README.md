# NYC-Marathon-Spectators-Calculator

A calculator for NYC Marathon spectators. Estimate when your runner will pass you on the course based on their splits.

## Streamlit Community Cloud

Use the app on [Streamlit Community Cloud](https://nyc-marathon-spectators-calculator.streamlit.app/).

## Instructions

1) **Select the runner's start wave** to set their start time.
2) **Enter the mile marker** where you are standing.
3) **Open the official [NYRR App](https://www.nyrr.org/get-involved/nyrr-app)** to find your runner’s most recent split time.
4) **Input the split** to calculate their estimated local time of arrival at your location.
